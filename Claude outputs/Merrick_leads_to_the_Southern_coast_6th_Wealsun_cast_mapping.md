## Point of the conversation
Having outrun the Earl's men-at-arms, Merrick leads the crew up off the beach into the windswept scrub on the **southern side of the island**, about two miles from the path, to a high vantage below the cliffs where they can see the lighthouse and the distant castle and can't be surprised. They lie low and hash out what just happened and what to do next:
- **Recriminations over the ruse:** Tyrus argues they should have let themselves be "captured" (he and Folsom playing drunk) so the two sneaks (Merrick and Gouge) could tail them and break them out later; Folsom defends giving the name "Owen," reasoning it might have bought them cover as "Owen's bodyguard."
- **Reading the enemy:** the men-at-arms are the **Earl of Redshore's** household troops. Folsom's **insight check** lands the key inference — the Earl wouldn't welcome Owen Black, because Owen's dealings left **six dead bodies in the chart-room bar** and mercenaries loose on the Earl's land. So the guard wanted "a discussion" with Owen, not a friendly meeting.
- **The plan:** rather than sneak into the Earl's castle, they lean toward seeking an **audience/parlay** with the **Earl of Redshore** to learn why he wanted Owen — with finding Owen Black himself floated as the alternative. They decide to **long-rest** in the wilderness and have Merrick guide them across the island toward the castle under cover of night/morning.
- **Closing beat:** Tyrus tells Gouge (who prefers to work alone) that the party has his back; Gouge accepts, dryly — "we're all in this shit together."

## Canon / continuity notes (important — political map)
- **The Earl of Redshore** rules this island; he is a **vassal of the Duke of Gratzel** ("Gretzel/Razzle" garbles), who is **at war with Monmurg**. So the Earl is at least nominally **allied with the party's enemies**, though there's historically little animosity between Monmurg and Redshore, and the Earl may not be happy about what's happening on his land. The **men-of-war in the bay are Gratzelian** (his liege's), which the crew resent. → Worth a worldbuilding/faction note.
- **Kane Tolley (Cain Toli)** is **from Toli** (part of the Sea Principalities), **not** Monmurg; there's "a weird marriage going on between Toli and Gratzel," and it's unknown how the Earl fits. Ties the Toli/Gratzel alliance thread.
- **Owen Black** is read here as a **self-serving middleman/traitor** "running shit back and forth," working with **Kane Tolley**, and knowing "a lot about that amulet with the turtle dragons" (callback to the Orb of the Dragon Turtle thread).
- **Appearance/cover:** the crew look like **mercenaries**, not Monmurg marines — Tyrus in a brass plate looted from a hobgoblin, Gouge in leather ("mercenary scumbag"), Merrick in ranger gear ("looks like a pilot"), not wearing colors. Relevant for how they'll pass at the castle.
