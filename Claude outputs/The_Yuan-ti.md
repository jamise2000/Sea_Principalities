# The Yuan-ti

A reference document, not an in-world artifact — this is the continuity ledger for the **Yuan-ti** ("the transformed"): humans remade into snake-people, their tie to the worship of **Insamiar the Black**, and their use as agents of the **Scarlet Brotherhood**. Compiled from the 5th-Wealsun dungeon scenes (`Jude_and_Paul_interrogate_prisoners`, `Jude_and_Paul_interrogation_cont`, `Jude_Paul_and_Leslie_collect_Alchemy_tomes`) and cross-checked against `Insamiar_the_Black.md`, `Name_Normalization_Key.md`, and the Scarlet Brotherhood material.

**Status on the page.** Unlike most of the Insamiar material, the Yuan-ti **have been dramatized**: two captured Scarlet-Brotherhood agents — a **male** and a **female** — are held in Paul's dungeon and interrogated on the **5th of Wealsun**. Jude tortures the male for venom and answers; the male delivers most of the lore below (as a captive cultist's claims, not neutral narration); the female is identified as a **magical leader** and held for later. This file records what was shown, marks cult-claim vs. observed fact, and flags the tensions to resolve.

**Naming (canon spelling).** **Yuan-ti** is the canonical form. The transcripts render it **"the Yontai"** (ASR); `Name_Normalization_Key.md` also logs "jaunty" / "yawn-tee" → **Yuan-ti**. In-world they are also called **"the transformed."**

## What they are

Per the DM's on-page account (`interrogate_prisoners`, 18–22): the Yuan-ti are **"people transformed into snakes with the minds of snakes"** — humans remade into serpent-people. The stated origin is that **"the transmuters from the east would do this to people to make them slaves."** Jude's late master, the alchemist **Albashon**, had **described creatures like this** — so they are known to learned alchemists, not a fresh mystery to the setting.

But the captured pair are **not** enslaved thralls: they are **Scarlet Brotherhood** agents who took the serpent-form **voluntarily**, as an act of worship (see below). This is the central tension of the entry (see Open Threads): the same transformation appears both as an **eastern transmuters' tool for making slaves** and as a **cult's chosen sacrament**.

## Physical form (observed)

From the male and female held in Paul's dungeon (`interrogate_prisoners`, 7–12):

- **Fangs**; **slit, snake-like eyes** ("their eyes are very much like yours" — said to Jude, an elf); the **beginnings of scales** apparent on the male's head.
- **Human-sized**, still roughly human in build, but "they don't look fully human." **They wear masks** in public precisely to hide the change ("It's obvious why they wear masks").
- **Fang venom:** a **pale-green liquid** milked from the fangs (`interrogate_prisoners`, 189–197) — Jude harvests it as a toxin sample. Whether this venom is the poison that killed Albashon (the **Gift of Insamiar** thread) or a related toxin is **not settled** (see Open Threads).
- **High pain tolerance**; even de-fanged and losing an eye, the male **hisses rather than screams** and is not visibly cowed (`interrogate_prisoners`, 33–35; `interrogation_cont`, 326).

## Behavior & the "minds of snakes"

- They **hiss, bite, and turn violent** — the guards keep them behind bars, hooded, crossbows ready (`interrogate_prisoners`, 4–5, 19).
- Despite "the minds of snakes," they **retain speech and intelligence**: the female curses coherently, and the male sustains a long, lucid theological argument under torture. Treat them as **coherent zealots**, not mindless beasts.
- **The female is a caster and was the leader** of the raid party (`collect_tomes`, 4–30): Jude judges her magical and orders her bound with **hands behind the back, fingertips laced, and gagged** — no somatic gestures, no verbal components. The male is watched but treated as the lesser threat.

## Speech & the Suel connection

The female curses in **Ancient Suel** — "You've failed, you foolish elf" (`interrogate_prisoners`, 65; ASR "Ancient Soul"). This fits the **Scarlet Brotherhood as a Suel secret society** (`Name_Normalization_Key.md`): these Yuan-ti are **Suel Brotherhood agents**, which threads the serpent-cult into the Suel-supremacist order rather than making it a purely marsh/kobold phenomenon.

## The snake-form and Insamiar (cult claim)

The male prisoner frames the transformation as **worship**, not mere disguise or enslavement (`interrogation_cont`):

- The serpent motif is **"Insamiar's call"** — **"in this form, we can hear [the dragon] better"** (131–133).
- **"All reptilians worship the dragon"** (189); the change is a **voluntary transformation** (198), which he contrasts with Jude's involuntary polymorph.
- The cult centers on **Insamiar**, an **ancient black dragon**, tied to the alchemical **nigredo** (ASR "the Negrado" — the blackening/dissolution/"cooking" stage), which the prisoner calls **"the god of cooking"** (154–167).
- **The Gift of Insamiar** is, in his telling, **"a liquid from the dragon's self,"** bringing **"insanity — or complete clarity… the passage into consciousness"** (351–364).

**Register note (per author ruling; `Insamiar_the_Black.md`):** the dragon-origin of the Gift is an **in-world rumor/cult claim, not confirmed narration**. Render the Yuan-ti's theology as *what they believe and assert*, not as authorial fact.

## Why they act (motive, on-page)

Under interrogation the male gives the Brotherhood's reasons (`interrogation_cont`, 349–387):

- They **assassinated Albashon** because he "knows more than most" and was **developing a counter to the Gift** — a substance to **abrogate its effects / ease those afflicted** before the coming war. They could not allow it.
- They know **Paul is trying to recreate the anti-sahaugin powder** — "the powder **Cain Toli** has taken from the Helm" — and mean to stop that too.
- He fixes on **Jude**: an elf with "colder eyes than any of the others," **"susceptible to the gift,"** who **"hears the dragon's call"** and will **"in time fall and succumb."** (A **Jude-corruption thread** to watch — see `interrogation_cont`.)

## How this connects to the wider Insamiar material

`Insamiar_the_Black.md` documents Insamiar's worship as a **marsh phenomenon** — the Black Kobolds and the human **Followers of Insamiar** in the Hool Marshes, under kobold **All-Watcher** leadership. The Yuan-ti give Insamiar a **second, urban, Suel face**: serpent-transformed **Scarlet Brotherhood** agents operating in **Monmurg**, far from the marsh, invoking the same dragon and the same "Gift." Whether the Brotherhood's serpent-cult is an **offshoot of the marsh faith**, a **parallel Suel tradition** that venerates the same dragon, or the **"eastern transmuters'" craft** turned to worship, is **open** (see below). Either way, it widens Insamiar's reach from a swamp cult into a **sophisticated conspiracy inside the Sea Principalities.**

## Open Threads (established but unresolved — do not silently close)

- **Enslaved thralls vs. willing devotees.** The DM's account says the Yuan-ti are humans **enslaved** by "eastern transmuters" and given "the minds of snakes"; the captives call their change a **voluntary act of worship**. Are these two different things (transmuted slave-serpents vs. self-transformed cultists), two accounts of one thing, or a cult that has embraced a craft first meant for enslavement? **Open — a significant reveal either way.**
- **The transformation's mechanism.** How a human is made Yuan-ti — the "eastern transmuters'" craft, a rite of the Gift, or something else — is unshown. Keep it in the setting's **alchemy-with-a-mystery** register if revealed.
- **Is the fang venom the Gift of Insamiar?** The **pale-green** fang toxin, the poison that killed Albashon, and the **Gift** ("a liquid from the dragon's self") may be one substance or several. Unsettled (ties to the terminology question in `Insamiar_the_Black.md`).
- **Brotherhood ↔ marsh cult.** Whether the Scarlet Brotherhood's serpent-cult is the marsh Followers' order, a parallel Suel tradition, or connected through the "eastern transmuters." Open.
- **"Hearing the dragon."** The claim that the serpent-form lets them "hear Insamiar better," and that Jude is "susceptible" and will "succumb," is cult assertion — but the **Jude-corruption thread** it plants should be tracked, not dropped.
- **The two captives.** The magical female leader and the de-fanged male ("Toothless") remain in Paul's custody — live assets for future interrogation/escape/leverage.
