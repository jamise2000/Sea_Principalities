## Point of the conversation
Merrick's turn at the morning debrief. He lays out what he understands and the DM sharpens it:
- **His mission** (from **Lord Jamis**): go with Owen and gather as much intelligence as he could about what's happening across the principalities.
- **What he's figured out:** **Cain Toli** holds an **artifact** (the DM corrects Merrick's "amulet") that **controls the turtle dragons**, which blockade the coast and cause the "mysterious storms." Redshore is the choke-point of an **embargo** — merchants routed here and unable to leave — and the **Monmurg fleet can't break it** because anything bound for Monmurg gets redirected/storm-blocked.
- **The DM's key nudge:** Cain Toli **headed east two days ago** (Merrick saw him), taking his turtle dragons with him — so **Redshore is currently vulnerable**: with the dragons gone, what stops the Monmurg fleet from wiping out the three men-of-war? The DM also reminds Merrick that, as a **ranger**, he can scan the coast for dragons (favored-terrain detection out to six miles) — the open problem is **getting word to Jamis**.
- **Conclusion:** the mission (find out what Owen knows) has **already succeeded** — Owen knew too much and told Folsom too much, which is exactly why he tried to have them killed (he realized Merrick was a spy). Best move: **get off Redshore and report to Jamis** before they're caught; they're behind enemy lines, and Gouge/Folsom/Tyrus all stick out.
