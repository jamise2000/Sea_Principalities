## Point of the conversation
On the dawn watch, Gouge and Merrick get on the same page — explicitly noting that **Tyrus** is along mainly to bring supplies back and ease Monmurg's shortages ("the meat shield"), which isn't *their* real errand. Their read:
- The **recon mission is done** — Owen told Folsom far too much, realized it, and tried to have them (recognized agents) killed to cover himself. The Earl's guards were incidental; the crew means nothing to them.
- **The move is to get back to Lord Jamis** with everything. Merrick, using his knowledge of the waters, can check **on the way out whether the turtle dragons are still holding the blockade** — and if Cain Toli took them east, the **Monmurg navy could sweep in, smash the three men-of-war, and reopen Redshore** without the crew even being present.
- **Bigger picture:** figure out what **Cain Toli** is doing — possibly take a fleet to his island or go after him.
- **Escape problem:** it's ~60 miles of open sea past a warship blockade; they'd need something small, at night, no lights. They kick around stealing a vessel or slipping away from the rocky/industrial side of the island. (Callback: last time they got off **Helm Island** on a homemade raft.)

## Canon / continuity notes (major)
- **The artifact's origin (new, important):** a **lich / necromancer made the artifact long ago**, and **made a deal with Cain Toli** — giving him the artifact **in exchange for breaking a magical ward on the coast of Salinmoor** ("Selenmor"). This is a significant new thread (the artifact's maker + a warded coast at Salinmoor) — likely worth its own worldbuilding note.
- Reinforces the plan-level intel: the turtle dragons (controlled via the artifact) are the blockade's teeth; if Cain Toli left with them, Redshore is exposed.
- **Jude & Paul** were last in **Monmurg** — they went to the palace after the crew got back "from the home"; the crew will rely on **Jamis** to coordinate whether they're needed.
- **Tyrus's motivation** distinguished from Gouge/Merrick's: he's focused on relief supplies for Monmurg, not the espionage.
