## Point of the conversation
Around noon, Merrick spends a ranger spell slot to scan the seas from Helm's deck. The **detection comes back clean of dragons**: no turtle dragons — indeed **no dragons at all** — anywhere in the channel between **Redshore and Keoland/Salinmoor** and roughly **two miles south of Redshore**. There *are* **sahaugin** in the water (not many; exact count unknown). The DM stresses this is **instantaneous, not permanent** knowledge — right now the dragon screen is gone ("they're all out of here"), confirming Cain Toli took them east. This is the confirmation the whole escape/navy plan hinged on. Merrick then turns to op-sec: he **hasn't told Helm about the dragons**, worries **Tyrus has been talking too much** to Helm, and resolves to "put Tyrus in check."
