## Point of the conversation
The crew wakes at 7 a.m. and quickly aligns: the recon is done, so the priority is **get back to Monmurg**, report to **Lord Jamis** (Gouge floats introducing the others to him), and send the **Monmurg navy** to break the blockade and bring supplies. Debating whether the turtle dragons are still in place, they map it out — Cain Toli was seen heading **east two days ago**, and Merrick can burn a ranger spell slot to **detect turtle dragons within six miles**, though he wants to save it until they have a ship. They walk the **abandoned south/west coast** looking for a boat and find nothing — the whaling town's ships are all corralled by the men-of-war — so they'll have to go into the **city**. Passing the grim whaling district (rendering vats, a beach of whale bones, the Earl's oil barrels), they head for the docks. Plans for disguise (Folsom's costume pack; hiding the conspicuous Tyrus; splitting into pairs — Gouge+Tyrus, Merrick+Folsom) get worked out. Folsom tries to lead them to **Owen's old sloop**, but his memory check is poor — and when they find the right dock (marked by the unlit lantern), **the boat is gone**. New wrinkle: **Keoland marines are now patrolling** the docks (not there before), so Folsom peels off to chat one up for information as the scene ends.
