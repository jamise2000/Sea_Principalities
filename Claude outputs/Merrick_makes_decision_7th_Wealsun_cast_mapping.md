## Point of the conversation
Merrick returns to the cabin with the all-clear (no dragons) and, deflecting Tyrus's dragon-fear so Helm won't learn about the ranger tracking, commits to **writing the coded letter**. Over a drink, **Captain Helm** ("call me Eric — Captain Eric Helm") chats up the crew and vents that he's been begging Jamis for weeks to send the fleet: the three men-of-war (400 marines each) could be **easily crushed by Monmurg's squadron**, so why does "the spider" say *wait*? Gouge and Tyrus theorize there must be a "hook" holding Monmurg back. Merrick attaches the letter to Helm's pigeon (Helm pointedly won't read it, and says a reply will come "**but not that way**," in a few days). Then Helm delivers the arc's **big reveal** — the rumor behind everything:

- The **Duke of Gradsul** wants a **pure-blood Suel wife**, rare even among Keoland's great houses.
- So Gradsul cuts a deal with **House Toli**: a woman of Toli's bloodline marries into Gradsul, and in return Gradsul helps Toli against **Lord Jamis** — an **alliance of House Toli and House Gradsul (House Rola)**, historic rivals now uniting.
- The target is **Prince Jeon** ("Gion"), the richest, most powerful of the princes, who stands in their way; with **Keoland's armies and Toli's ports**, they'd have a staging ground to march on Monmurg. The blockade/starvation of Monmurg is meant to **cut Jeon down a notch**. The Outer Isles princes would never accept it.

This reframes the whole campaign: the embargo isn't about commerce, it's a **dynastic-marriage power play against Prince Jeon**.

## Canon / continuity notes (major)
- **Captain's full name: Eric Helm** ("call me Eric").
- **THE MOTIVE (rumor, via Helm):** Duke of Gradsul seeks a pure-blood **Suel** wife → marriage-alliance with **House Toli** → combined move to weaken **Prince Jeon** (the richest prince); Keoland armies + Toli ports = a path to march on Monmurg. Blockade = pressure on Jeon. → Strong candidate for a worldbuilding note (the political spine of Book Two).
- **House Rola** = the Duke of Gradsul's house (Keoland); long rivals of **House Toli**. Keoland has "three or four" great (Suel) houses.
- Helm confirms **Monmurg's navy could easily break the blockade** (men-of-war = 400 marines each), deepening the mystery of why **Jamis holds the fleet** — a shrewd tactician with hidden reasons.
- **Comms:** the coded pigeon letter goes to Jamis; a reply will come by a **different channel** in a few days.
- Reinforces **Prince Jeon = "Gion" = the richest man**, the linchpin the enemies want removed.
