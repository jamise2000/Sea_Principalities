# Torus

A large Suel man of Port Toli, an agent of the Toli — and of Cain Toli in particular — who runs the family's business at Redshore and handles the smuggler Owen Black. Cold, careful, and knowing where Owen is reckless. Introduced 6th Wealsun (Book Two). An NPC.

**Name note:** *Torus* (this NPC) is a near-homophone of the player character **Tyrus** (the paladin) and the raw transcripts confuse the two constantly ("Taurus," "Tyrius," even "Tyrus"). Keep them distinct. `Owen_shows_his_face_6th_Wealsun` is the transcript where Torus is introduced (see `worldbuilding/Name_Normalization_Key.md`).

## Physical Description

A **large man, plainly of Port Toli** — Suel by look and bearing. He keeps to a cabin aboard a boat at Redshore, favors the black Port Toli rum, and carries himself with a measured composure that sets him apart from the loud, reckless Owen Black.

## Role & History

Torus is **Owen Black's Toli handler** and the party who receives what Owen brings in — introduced when Owen leads Folsom aboard to "meet a friend," intending to drug Folsom with the gift of Insamiar and ship him to **Cain Toli's island**. When Owen bungles it and doses himself instead, Torus is left holding the situation, and his handling of it shows the man:

- He is **inside the Toli's secrets.** He speaks of "the stone" — the artifact (the Orb) — as rumored to have been made by **the Lich Acerak** (in truth the transmuter **Ujor Udias**, who took the vanished lich's name — see `The_Orb_of_the_Dragon_Turtle.md`), knowledge the Toli got from "the necromancer in the swamp" (Ixid / the Sons of Olan). He knows what the gift of Insamiar is and does, and had warned Owen it was dangerous, "not something to be given lightly."
- He is **coldly pragmatic.** Rather than cross swords with the escaped Monmurg party himself — he doesn't even know their faces — he plans to send word (and coin) to the **Earl of Redshore**, and let Redshore's men-at-arms and, if it comes to it, the Navy deal with "smugglers from Monmurg." He judges that Owen, once the gift of Insamiar takes hold, is near the end of his usefulness — "best keep him alive, for now."
- He grasps the larger game: that these are Lord Jamis's Monmurg spies, that Redshore and Monmurg can be set against each other "on paper and in reality," and that neither side sees how far it is being manipulated.

## Personality & Motivation

Disciplined, patient, and loyal to the Toli interest — Cain Toli is the benefactor "who had given him much, and he expected more from." He treats people (Owen included) as assets to be spent, prefers manipulation and cutouts to open violence, and is the steady, calculating counterweight to Owen's bravado.
