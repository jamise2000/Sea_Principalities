# Minor Characters

A roster of named-but-minor characters — their names, positions, affiliations, status, and where they appear — who don't (yet) warrant their own profile. Spellings follow `worldbuilding/Name_Normalization_Key.md`. Promote any entry to its own file if it grows in importance.

## Roster

### Rodiger
- **Position:** Commander of the **North Gate garrison to the Foreign District** (Monmurg).
- **Affiliation:** Monmurg city guard / garrison.
- **Status:** Alive.
- **Appears:** Book Two, 4th Wealsun — Paul's sequence around the alchemist's shop fire (`Paul_goes_to_fetch_formula`, `Paul_hurries_toward_the_fire`, `Paul_organizes_guards`, `Paul_examines_Alchemists_body`).
- **Note:** ASR sometimes renders other spellings; canonical is **Rodiger**.

### Captain Nado
- **Position:** A captain of the **Monmurg navy** (deceased).
- **Affiliation / relations:** **Owen Black's uncle and former commander**; also **Merrick's father** — consistent, since Owen and Merrick are cousins.
- **Status:** **Dead.**
- **Appears (referenced):** Book Two, 5th Wealsun — Owen's interior recollection (`Owens_suspicians`). Nado denied Owen a naval commission, citing his recklessness, and told him he leaned too much toward "Captain Cross's" attributes and needed to learn humility before he'd earn command.
- **Note:** ASR renders "Nato"/"NATO"; canonical is **Nado**.

### Albashon
- **Position:** A master **alchemist and transmuter** of Monmurg's **Foreign District**; old, of Suel descent; keeper and reader of the ***Magnum Opus* of Ujor Udias**; widely consulted on alchemy.
- **Affiliation / relations:** Independent scholar-alchemist; **mentor to Leslie** (took him on despite his being Oeridian).
- **Status:** **Dead** — killed in the **Scarlet Brotherhood's** night raid on his shop (Book Two, 4th Wealsun).
- **Appears:** 4th Wealsun — the "Alchemist" of `Jude_and_Paul_visit_Alchemist`, `Jude_in_Alchemy_shop`, `Jude_combat`, `Leslies_introduction`, and `Paul_examines_Alchemists_body`; named "Albashon" in `Leslies_introduction`.
- **Appearance:** Extremely old; recognizably human but otherwise hard to place, **mole-like, with large spectacles**. Greets customers with a doubled phrase ("Can I help you, can I help you!"). A **miser**: he hates parting with gold, though he gives his work away freely to the poor (`worldbuilding/Albashons_Journal.md`, ruling 15). He thinks in alchemical symbols when strangers are near, so a mind-reader sees sigils instead of thoughts (`worldbuilding/Albashons_Journal.md`, 15th of Readying, 576 CY).
- **Note:** The cast files label him "Alchemist (NPC)"; his name is **Albashon**. He is the "Alchemist" throughout the 4th-Wealsun sequence. His spellbook, annotated *Magnum Opus* and journal are in `worldbuilding/Albashons_Knowledge_Box.md` and `worldbuilding/Albashons_Journal.md`.
- **Secret knowledge:** One of the very few who know that **Zafar Azane** and his sister (Lady Jamis) descend from the vanished great Suel house **Azani** — and he knows **Paul's** lineage far better than Paul does (Book 2 Ch. 8: "You do not use the name Azani?").
- **Note:** The cast files label him "Alchemist (NPC)"; his name is **Albashon**. He is the "Alchemist" throughout the 4th-Wealsun sequence.

### Captain Cross
- **Position:** A ship's captain — a **historical figure** of Monmurg's past.
- **Status:** **Dead** (historical).
- **Appears (referenced):** Book Two, 5th Wealsun — `Owens_suspicians`, where the late **Captain Nado** warned young Owen Black that he "tended more toward the attributes of Captain Cross" than Nado liked, invoking Cross as a cautionary example a would-be commander should not emulate.
- **Note:** Recorded here for reference only; not an active character.

### Mother Tansy
- **Position:** An old **eel-seller** at the mouth of the **Warren** (Foreign District, Monmurg).
- **Affiliation / relations:** Long-time (and habitually cheating) supplier to **Albashon**; later his go-between, carrying his white draught to Gift victims in the Warren.
- **Status:** Unknown. Afflicted by the Gift (torpor) from Wealsun 575 CY. She did not come for her bottles on the morning of the 4th of Wealsun, 576 CY.
- **Appears:** `worldbuilding/Albashons_Journal.md` (Wealsun 575 – Wealsun 576 CY). Her grandson also appears there.

### Harrud the Chandler
- **Position:** A **chandler** who sells Albashon sulfur, saltpeter and other stores.
- **Status:** Alive (presumed).
- **Appears:** `worldbuilding/Albashons_Journal.md` (571 CY ledger).
- **Note:** Passes off wet local saltpeter as Keoish.

### The rope-mender and his wife
- **Position:** **Warren** residents. The rope-mender insists his children have been swapped. His wife gives Albashon the name "the Gift … Insamiar's" (20th of Fireseek, 576 CY).
- **Status:** Alive (as of Fireseek 576 CY); both afflicted by the Gift.
- **Appears:** `worldbuilding/Albashons_Journal.md`. Unnamed.

### Seebo Beren
- **Position:** A **gnome alchemist**; the student of **Edium Nicond**, the master transmuter of Keoland (dead by Flocktime 576 CY).
- **Affiliation / relations:** Known to **Lord Phranck** (`Jude_Leslie_escape_scarlet_brotherhood_4th_Wealsun`: "He does have a student ... I know that Lord Frank knows him"). Albashon's dying words name him as someone who could help Paul. In the author's account of Phranck's visit to Albashon (15th of Readying, 576 CY), Seebo is the one who identified the **"red dust"**, the supposed Philosopher's Stone, in **Archibald Fryhone's journal**. That is why Phranck asked Albashon about it.
- **Status:** "Gone quiet" after Nicond's death (Gradsul letter, 8th of Flocktime, 576 CY). Albashon met him once, years earlier.
- **Appears (referenced):** `worldbuilding/Albashons_Journal.md`; Albashon's dying words (4th Wealsun).
- **Note:** "Archibald" is **Archibald Fryhone** (author ruling): the naturalist who wrote `Guide_to_the_Hool_Marshes.md` (CY 554) and the Archibald of Grayson Jamis's letters (Book One Ch. 7), who hunted the original *Magnum Opus*. They are one man.

### Archibald Fryhone
- **Position:** A **naturalist and scholar**; author of the Hool Marshes tome (`worldbuilding/Guide_to_the_Hool_Marshes.md`, CY 554).
- **Affiliation / relations:** Friend of **Grayson Jamis**, whose letters mention him warmly (Book One Ch. 7). He was relentless in pursuing the true, unedited ***Magnum Opus***, which he believed was hidden in the dungeons of the **Office of Forbidden Magic** in Gradsul, and he drew Grayson into that pursuit. His **journal** is where **Seebo Beren** identified the **"red dust"**, the supposed Philosopher's Stone (the lead behind Lord Phranck's question to Albashon, 15th of Readying, 576 CY).
- **Status:** Unknown.
- **Appears (referenced):** Book One Ch. 7, 9 (as "Archibald"); `worldbuilding/Guide_to_the_Hool_Marshes.md` (as author).
- **Note:** Author ruling: the "Archibald" of Grayson's letters and Archibald Fryhone are **the same man**.
