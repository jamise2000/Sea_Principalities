# Leslie

A young Oeridian sage-turned-wizard of Monmurg — apprentice and lab-assistant to the master alchemist **Albashon** — who fled the Scarlet Brotherhood's night raid on his master's shop alongside Jude, surfacing through a sewer manhole in Blood Alley behind Ferd's Bar. Introduced 4th Wealsun (Book Two).

**Player character** (newly introduced in Book Two). Leslie's backstory is established in a full character-creation session, `transcripts/.../Leslies_introduction_4th_Wealsun`.

## Background

Leslie is **Oeridian**, born in **Monmurg** to a fairly wealthy **merchant family** — but he never wanted the family trade. He was always a **sage**: bookish, sharp, and given access as a young man to the city's better libraries (the Scholars' District), which he exhausted. He is not a noble.

His path into magic came through one man: **Albashon**, an exceptional and very old **Suel alchemist and transmuter** with a workshop in Monmurg's Foreign District (see the minor-characters roster). Most Suel will not take Oeridian apprentices, and few Oeridians ever become wizards — the Suel guard the arts, and ancient Suel is "the language of magic" — but Albashon was unbiased, recognized Leslie's talent, and took him on. Over several years Leslie served as his **lab assistant**, learned to distill chemicals and work alchemy from Albashon's copy of the ***Magnum Opus* of Ujor Udias**, and was taught to read ancient Suel and to cast spells. Albashon gave Leslie his **spellbook** and, in Leslie's own reckoning, made him the wizard he is.

He is a **wizard** in the learned, studious sense (knowledge and technique, not a sorcerer's innate charisma), and physically no fighter — during the escape he is described as "pretty weak," needing Jude to take the lead.

## Story Entry (4th Wealsun)

The **Scarlet Brotherhood** struck Albashon's shop by night, killing the master alchemist. Leslie escaped the raid **with Jude**, the two of them going down into the tunnels/sewers beneath the Foreign District, Jude cleaning the filth off them with a cantrip, and climbing out a manhole into **Blood Alley — the dangerous cut behind Ferd's Bar** that Leslie knows well. He emerges attached to Jude and, presumably, drawn into the party's orbit from here.
