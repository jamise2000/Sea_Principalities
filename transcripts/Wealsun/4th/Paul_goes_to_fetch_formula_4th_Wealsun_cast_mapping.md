## The point of the conversation
Runs **parallel to Jude & Leslie's escape** (same night). Paul walks from the alchemist's shop up through the foreign district (the Warren, God's Row) to the **North Gate**, where the guard — **Rodiger** — lets him through the side gate and lets slip a **rumor that Paul was on Helm Island** (a friend at the Royal Docks saw his silver mask). Paul shuts it down. At the palace he collects the **powder packet** and **Grayson's notes** on the compound (copper sulfate — leaving the three kegs and the Manual of the Homunculus). Returning, he learns the guard's name (**Rodiger**), then sees **bursts of flame ~1,000 ft away in the Warren** — recognizes **Jude's evoker fireball** (the alchemist's shop under attack). He has Rodiger send a **fire brigade/squad** and sprints toward the burning building.
