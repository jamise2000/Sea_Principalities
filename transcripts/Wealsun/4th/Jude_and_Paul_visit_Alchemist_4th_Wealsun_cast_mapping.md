## The point of the conversation
Jude takes Paul down to the foreign district (8 pm) to see **Albashon**, the alchemist he's been cultivating and to whom he gave a sample of the Helm-Island anti-sahaugin powder. Albashon recognizes Paul — knows his family name isn't "Rivero" but **Azani** (the Zafar Azane line), and asks after his grandfather, implying a prior relationship with the family. Albashon reports his analysis: the yellow powder is **copper + sulfur** combined in a process he's never seen (with trace elements he can't identify); it's inert to humans/elves but shreds a fish's gills — he demonstrates on a goldfish (a thousandth of a pinch is lethal). He can't reproduce it; the structure eludes him. It's non-explosive except pressurized or wet-and-aged (exothermic in seawater). Paul has **Grayson's equations** but not on him; he leaves (sending his homunculus / heading back to the palace) to fetch them while Jude stays to guard Albashon, whom Jude genuinely fears will be targeted.
