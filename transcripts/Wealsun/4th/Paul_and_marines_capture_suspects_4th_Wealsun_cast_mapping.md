## The point of the conversation
Paul and **Sergeant Holt**'s marines storm the Scarlet Brotherhood safehouse. Inside: wretched addicted cultists (hollow-eyed, hair and teeth falling out — the Warren's "meth heads") plus two burly Brotherhood fighters and the woman. A brutal fight — one Brotherhood man is killed, the other man and the woman subdued (she's monstrously tough, biting and hissing). Paul orders prisoners **taken alive for interrogation**. **The reveal (561–574):** a marine pulls the mask off the captured man — fang-like teeth, angular bone-shaped face, and **vertical-slit "snake eyes" — explicitly likened to Jude** (a Yuan-ti). Paul sends the prisoners to the **Royal Dungeon**, thanks Holt's marines (promises an ale barrel for the **4th Wharf Marine Barrack**), and they file out through the Warren with the two prisoners in irons.

- **The Scarlet Brotherhood agent has "snake eyes like Jude"** (565–574) — the Yuan-ti trait is visually tied to Jude; render deliberately (foreshadowing Jude's own nature/lineage thread).
