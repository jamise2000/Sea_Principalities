## The point of the conversation
Drawn by Jude's fireballs, Paul sneaks toward the burning alchemist shop, climbs to the rooftops, and **eavesdrops on three Scarlet Brotherhood agents** in a nearby building. Their conversation confirms the night's events: **the old man (Albashon) is dead** ("died from your dagger blow"), **the elf (Jude) and the young noble escaped into the sewers**, "**their two compatriots followed them**," and there may be **knowledge the elf now carries** — so the killing did not secure the secret. The agents decide to **sleep there and wait**. Paul weighs options (torch the Warren with his powder vial; storm them with guards; observe) but rules the pursuers "not of immediate concern," returns to the fire, finds **Rodiger's brigade** dousing the chemical blaze, and moves to rally troops.
