## The point of the conversation
Climbing up out of the dungeon after the lesson, Jude and Paul argue and banter the whole way. Paul needles Jude about being an elf (does he sleep, is he depressed, is he "gonna end the world"); the DM paints Jude as immortally young, carrying enormous melancholy in a perfect face. Jude flips it — lecturing Paul that his emotional "thing" (dead mother, unloving father) will get him killed as a mage, and that as his pupil he must take the poking. They re-litigate the Helm captive, Paul asks when he learns levitate (the spell that saved him from the shark), and it closes on Paul's temper — the DM: Jude isn't sure Paul even has tear ducts.
