The day began just after midnight. Paul had come back to Monmurg Palace with the prisoners from the raid on the Scarlet Brotherhood's hideout. He had each prisoner put in a separate cell, and the two real Brotherhood agents went into the most secure ones, with five palace guards keeping crossbows on them instead of the usual one. Those two were violent and kept trying to bite, so they had to be hooded. The six others taken from the building were half-starved locals in a dreamlike daze. Paul spent an hour trying to question them and got nothing except one man muttering "the dragon, the dragon" before he drifted off again. Paul had the six dumped back outside, sent a runner to the north gate to watch for Jude, and went to his study to read a book on torture.

At the same time, Jude had finished his four-hour trance and recovered his spells. He woke Leslie, the dead alchemist's apprentice, at 1 a.m. on the couch in his room above Ferd's bar. Leslie was rattled, and he blamed Jude for the fire. Jude admitted he had been "a little overzealous" with his fire spells, but said you don't hold back against assassins, and reminded Leslie of the black ooze that had poured from the old man's eyes. Jude gave Leslie a hooded cloak to hide his face. The two slipped past the drunken crowd at Ferd's and walked the well-lit Row of the Gods to the burned shop. A sergeant of the Upper City guard turned them away "by order of Paul Revero" and would not say whether Paul was there. Jude decided against sneaking in through the Warrens and walked to the palace instead, reaching the north gate at 1:30. The guards there recognized "the wizard."

In Paul's study the two traded stories. Paul had watched the fire flare up several times from across the city, found the shop gutted, and followed the masked agents to a building less than a block away. They had made no effort to hide, and he took them with a handful of Marines. He believed he had recovered the alchemist's body. Jude passed on what Albashon told him before he died: the old man was killed by a poison called the Gift of Insamiar, not by the stab wound that took his eye. Jude also said the anti-sahuagin powder Paul needs could not be recreated from memory, so they would have to search the ruins for a fireproof strongbox. Leslie babbled about being "the man of the myths" and told Paul his favorite color was red, "you just don't know it yet." Paul complained that Jude kept bringing strangers into his palace.

At 2 a.m. they went down to the dungeons. In the torchlight Jude saw that the two prisoners had been changed: fangs, slit pupils like his own, and the start of scales on the male's head. Jude recalled his old master Absalon describing such people as Yuan-ti, humans turned into snakes with snakes' minds by eastern transmuters. When Jude insulted the female, she answered in Ancient Suel: "You've failed, you foolish elf." The jailer brought a man-catcher pole, and the guards wrestled the male into chains and onto a chair in a bare room with a floor drain. Jude pried his jaws open and milked a pale green venom from his fangs into sample vials. Leslie kept arguing that the prisoners should be made "workers" rather than killed.

Jude then pulled out both fangs with pliers. He was clumsy at first, muttering to himself as he worked, and even the jailer felt queasy. Then he burned the man's hand. The prisoner barely flinched and kept hissing. When Jude asked why the snake form, the prisoner said it was "Insamiar's call," which they heard more clearly in this shape. Jude recalled the legend that Insamiar is an ancient black dragon tied to the alchemical stage of boiling and breaking down. The prisoner then turned on Jude. He told Paul that the elf could also hear the dragon's call, that Jude's eyes were "colder than any I've seen," and he asked why an elf would live among humans. After Jude scooped out one of the prisoner's eyes with a spoon, the man gave the motive for the killing. Albashon had been working on a treatment for the Gift and had been taking pain relief to its victims. The Brotherhood also knew Paul was trying to recreate the yellow powder that Cain Toli took from the *Helm*, the powder that protects Monmurg, and they meant to stop it.

Around 3 a.m. the three talked privately in the dungeon corridor, and Leslie revealed that his master kept a "knowledge box" in a stone compartment under his bed that only he knew how to open. A long, comic negotiation followed. Leslie wanted ten minutes alone with "his inheritance." Paul demanded any notes on the plague and the powder, and offered Leslie a laboratory, money and ownership of the recipe if he would mass-produce it for the royal family. Paul also warned that the powder would be made "with or without you," and threatened to put Leslie in the chair and pull his teeth. Leslie talked like a would-be industrialist and asked to meet Paul's uncle, the richest man in the Flaness. In the end he agreed that they would all open the box together.

Before they left, Jude ordered the female prisoner, whom he took for the leader and a spellcaster, gagged with her hands bound behind her and her fingers laced together with cord, so she could make no spoken words or gestures. Neither prisoner was to be fed for a day or two. Paul's guards saluted him through the cordon at 3:30. On the way into the ruin, Jude retold the attack in full: the stab through the door slit, a fireball, a wall of fire to cover the escape down the spiral stair, Albashon dying of the poison in the sewers, and two pursuers killed in the water. In the bedroom, Leslie said a magic word and opened the box. Inside were three books, about eight scrolls and a set of small potions. Jude loaded it all onto his floating disc, covered with a cloak.

Paul brought them back to his own shabby wing of the palace, where the narration describes him as the disfigured bastard son of Prince Jeon's dead sister. Behind a hidden door at the back of his closet, a passage ran down through the cliff, past old arrow-slit guard rooms from the days when pirates held Monmurg, to a secret apartment with a bedroom and a fully equipped alchemical lab. Leslie tried to buy the place and demanded ten bodyguards before he would sleep anywhere else. Paul refused to let him live there. The exhausted Paul went to bed and left Jude and Leslie shut in the hidden lab to study the books for the recipe. The entrance was effectively blocked, and Leslie joked that he would wake Paul later to report Jude had set the lab on fire making pancakes.
