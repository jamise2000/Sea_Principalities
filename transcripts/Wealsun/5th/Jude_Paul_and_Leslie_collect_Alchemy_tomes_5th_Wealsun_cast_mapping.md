## Point of the conversation
At ~3 a.m. the party locks down the prisoners — **Jude** dictates anti-caster measures for the **magical female** (bind hands behind the back, lace the fingertips, gag her: no somatic or verbal components) — then the three head out to the burnt alchemist shop. **Jude recaps the attack** for Paul: a knock ~15–20 minutes after Paul left, the master **stabbed in the face** at the door and **poisoned** ("that black dragon, Sam[iar]"), Jude dragging him, discovering the hidden assistant (**Leslie**), casting a **wall of fire** to cover their escape into the sewers — the master dying of the poison, not the wound. At the ruin (stone-built, roof collapsed), **Jude casts Light** and **Leslie** leads them to the master's bedroom, where he opens the **hidden stone box** with a secret magic word — pulling out **~3 books, ~8 scrolls, and a cache of potions** (no money). Jude conjures a **Tenser's Floating Disc**, covers the haul with a cloak, and they take it back up. Paul leads them through a **secret door in his closet** down into a forgotten **cliff-side fortification** (old pirate arrow-slits over the bay) to a **hidden apartment with a laboratory** — where he'll stash the research and (exhausted) leaves **Leslie and Jude** to pore over the tomes for the powder recipe.
