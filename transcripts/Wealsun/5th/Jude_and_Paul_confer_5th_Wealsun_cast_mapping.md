## Point of the conversation
Stepping into the hallway at 3 a.m., the three confer on their next move. **Paul** wants the alchemist's research — specifically any notes on the **"black plague" (the Gift/Insamiar) spreading in the interior** and on the **anti-sahuagin powder** ("the white/yellow powder") — and reveals (via the DM) that Albashon kept a **hidden stone "knowledge box" in a secret compartment under his bed**. **Leslie**, as the master's **heir**, insists the box and its papers are *his* inheritance and demands first/private access; a long, comic negotiation follows in which Paul offers Leslie a **laboratory/tower, funding, and proprietorship over the powder's manufacture** (backed by the royal family) in exchange for the notes, while **Jude** needles them both and disclaims interest in the "couple silver pieces." Paul makes the stakes blunt: the powder **will be manufactured with or without Leslie** — cooperate and profit, or end up "six feet in the ground." They agree to **all go together to the burnt shop and open the box** (Leslie keeps the gold/silver and gets to skim the personal papers). They send the fang-pulled prisoner ("**Toothless**") back to his cell and head off — the DM noting Monmurg is the richest city in the Flanaess and **Paul's uncle the richest man in it**.
