## Point of the conversation
Alone in his cabin after the dragon talk, Owen realizes he was played: Folsom's flattery pumped him for the secret of Cain Toli's control over the turtle dragons. Watching Merrick and Folsom whisper on deck, he concludes Folsom is an agent of **Lord Jamis and Prince Jeon**, and that Merrick — long favored by Jamis, whose late father **Captain Nado** once denied Owen a naval commission for recklessness — is here on a spying mission, with Tyrus and Folsom as his (possibly unwitting) compatriots. He fixes on **Gouge as the linchpin** (and recalls Gouge once beat him senseless). Feeling cornered — "the product" to be bought and sold — he pours the black rum and resolves to reach Redshore, protect his business, and above all **keep Cain Toli's secret from Lord Jamis**, weighing imprisonment or death for the crew if it comes to it.
