It's become something of a stalemate — neither side able to leave the cover of their respective buildings.

TYRUS: Is this hobgoblin dead, the one we knocked down?

DM: Does anyone want to keep him alive — anyone speak Hobgoblin?

DM: [resolving a survival check] He survives — the sergeant takes him prisoner. The others are dispatched.

GOUGE: The big one's still alive, then?

GOUGE: Plenty left — let's move now.

TYRUS: I want everyone together for a second — quick plan. I'll go up the ladder, bang on the door, draw their attention. Whoever can scale the wall on the far side, away from the archers, do it while they're distracted by the door.

GOUGE: I'll go.

FOLSOM: I'll try the same on the other side.

TYRUS: No — there are archers covering that side. Come around this way instead.

DM: All right — Gouge and Folsom move to scale opposite sides of the wall while Tyrus draws attention at the main door.

TYRUS: [banging with his shield] I'll hammer on this and make as much noise as possible.

DM: You climb, athletics checks for both — [resolving] you both make good progress up the wall.

GOUGE: I want to go up as stealthily as possible — don't want a volley of arrows the moment they spot someone cresting the wall.

DM: [resolving stealth checks] Both of you make it to the top quietly.

GOUGE: [in Common] Surrender — we've taken the tower!

DM: One responds in broken Common.

DM (as hobgoblin): We surrender! [throws down his sword]

GOUGE: [climbing over anyway, attacking immediately]

DM: [resolving] You strike — wounds him badly, doesn't drop him outright.

TYRUS: [following up]

DM: [resolving multiple exchanges between Gouge, Tyrus, and the remaining hobgoblins] One of them draws a dagger despite the apparent surrender and lunges — you take a few points of damage blocking with your buckler, but you finish him off with a follow-up strike. GOUGE: [grim satisfaction] That's the two of them dealt with.

DM: Both hobgoblins are down, along with the earlier four. You loot them and clear the area.

GOUGE: Can we open the hatch and head down?

DM: Absolutely.

TYRUS: [kicking the hatch three times] Clear!

GOUGE: We need to signal that ship somehow — flaming arrow, anything.

TYRUS: Who's got the best ranged weapon for this?

GOUGE: Longbow's got better range than a heavy crossbow — maybe two hundred feet more.

FOLSOM: Let's get something burning and shoot it toward the ship.

GOUGE: I've got a tinderbox.

FOLSOM: [digging through his entertainer's pack] Let me see if I've got anything useful for color or visibility.

FOLSOM: [rolling] Sixteen total.

FOLSOM: I've got a twist of copper filings in my kit already — that'll give it color.

GOUGE: [firing] Up and out toward the ship — not down into the water.

TYRUS: [watching] Does it react — peel off, keep docking?

GOUGE: Good job, kid.

GOUGE: That's fantastic — and also terrible, since we're supposed to be protecting that guy.

GOUGE: I don't think we should risk going out the front with wounded among us.
