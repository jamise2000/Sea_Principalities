DM (as Fairwind): "Don't worry, they'll never be able to get through the defenses here. Your hobgoblins will be able to hold them off. If you think this attack will succeed — obviously it will not."

DM (as Toli noble): "We're not worried. In fact, we think we have most of what we need from the caves below. It seems, Lord Fairwind, that you've taken most of the chemicals for yourself. We'll have the rest — we'll take them to our island." [The other Toli stays silent.]

DM (as Fairwind): "Our deal is still in place, then? You will vouch for me on the council, pressuring Prince Jeon to give me more of the profits from his trade with the rest of the trader nations."

DM (as Toli noble): "Yes, of course — you'll be well compensated. Once this pressure on Prince Jeon ends, everything will go back to normal between us. We'll return all of the barrels of the... pest control, as it were, and you can go about your business. Until then, we'll store it at our location."

PAUL: What do I realize — are you asking me, like, what do I think about the chemicals?

There was an alliance between them, and that's what caused all this to happen. You're not entirely sure of the details, but obviously this was part of it.
