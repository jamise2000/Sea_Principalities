[Speaker Key: DM = Dungeon Master (narration) | GOUGE = Gouge's player | FOLSOM = Folsom's player. NOTE: "America" appearing in the source is almost certainly a mis-transcription of "Aristotle," consistent with its use elsewhere in these transcripts.]

DM: Third of Wealsun, as the sun sets in the west. Gouge walks Folsom out of the bar and into an alleyway, and sits him down to have a little talk.

GOUGE: You know what you're trying to do? Did you like the song?

FOLSOM: Yeah, fucking loved it, actually.

GOUGE: I saw it didn't go over well — reading the room, I took it upon myself to get your ass out of there the best way I could. Everyone was kind of stunned, looking at you, so I made a bigger scene — pulled a little blade, grabbed you, and hauled you out. I gave Tyrus a look, because they know he was with you, and told him to sit the fuck down — just so it's known that's being handled, in case there was any bad blood from this. There was a near blowup, but this was me pulling you out because I didn't want it to get any worse.

FOLSOM: And here I thought I was... making a life for myself.

GOUGE: Yeah, no — you guys do have maybe a little bit of a preconceived notion of who I am, or what I am. But remember when I shouted out and you thought I was the asshole? That's Jude. Okay? I follow the law. There's no reason — you have no reason to fear me, unless you cross somebody besides me that I'm loyal to. That's the only thing you should fear.

FOLSOM: Fair enough. Well, I appreciate you getting me out of there.

GOUGE: I do, and I want them here for this next part.

DM: He sends for Tyrus. So Tyrus walks out, and you find the two of them in the alleyway — Gouge actually has his arm around Folsom's shoulder and is talking to him, not in a mean way. You're not sure if it's a good sign or not.

TYRUS: [seeing him come up, throws up his knuckles] Uh-huh. [thinking] Okay, this is the part where I get stabbed.

GOUGE: I'll tell you, Tyrus — great song, but that's crap, brother. Yeah, I read the room and pulled them out. That was all for show, to get them out — I told them it was a joke, and "Bottoms up" cures more fear. I think that'll cure a lot. You guys don't have business in the barracks, do you?

TYRUS: No, let's [meet] over at the bar.

GOUGE: Can you go grab Merrick? I want a word with him. We'll be here — come back and we'll clear these guys after what just happened, and go have a couple.

TYRUS: Do I need to come back to you?

DM: You head back, and actually, when you walk in, you see Merrick shaking sand off and moving toward the door, so you catch him as he steps outside.

GOUGE: Did you see something in private? [to Merrick] Yeah — you two, just step out.

DM: [to Folsom] Let's see — basically, he's got something. A cloak. There's a green stone here.

GOUGE: It's a gem — you've got here. I don't use this as it should be used. But this thing... it's probably going to make your songs just a little bit better.

FOLSOM: Understood.

GOUGE: But this comes with a price, if you accept it. The price being — if we're together, in combat, and stuff's happening, and you're going to play your tunes, or whatever you're going to do to benefit somebody — I want you to look at me first before you decide. And I'm fair, and you can trust me. It's up to you whether you believe that or not. But listen: when you're going to throw out something to help somebody, or do something, I want you to look at me. If I give a nod, like, "no" — that means I don't need shit, and that's all on you. But if you look at me and I'm like [gesture] — that means whatever you're doing, you'd better fucking help me. Understood?

FOLSOM: You okay with that deal? I'm more than okay with that.

GOUGE: All right. I owe you one — or a few.

FOLSOM: We'll see — hopefully not, but maybe it's not that lucky. Or maybe some red eye.

GOUGE: Forges it is. All right — good kid.
