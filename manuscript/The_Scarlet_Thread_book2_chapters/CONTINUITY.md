# Book Two — Continuity Dependencies

A reference index, not an in-world artifact. Book Two shares its world, characters, and canon with Book One; the authoritative ledgers still live at the project root (`../../worldbuilding/`, `../../characters/`) and govern both books. This file only points to the specific canon Book Two leans on, so a continuity pass on these seven chapters knows what to check without re-reading all of Book One. When an edit here changes an established fact, update the root ledger in the same pass — this file is an index, not a second source of truth.

## Worldbuilding this book depends on

- **`The_Anti-Sahaugin_Powder.md`** — load-bearing for **Ch. 7 (*Copper and Sulfur*)**. Hold to: the powder is copper and sulfur combined by a lost method; **far more dangerous to water-breathers than to air-breathers, but NOT harmless to either** — diluted in the sea it is safe to swim, but the **raw powder is deadly to inhale** (Paul's mother's warning, Book One Ch. 6, Ch. 31; Paul warned Tyrus, Book One Ch. 38). The alchemist's "no harm at all" is reframed as diluted-in-water safety, with **Paul supplying the inhalation caveat**. Figures: one barrel ≈ ten square miles for ~ten days; **twenty-five** barrels in all — **four** rafted home, **twenty-one** left in the grotto (Ch. 1–2); supply cut off **~forty years** ago, not common knowledge (Jamis, Ch. 1; Jude to Albashon, Ch. 7). Not ordinarily explosive, but volatile under pressure or after aging in seawater.
- **`magic_system.md`** — load-bearing for **Ch. 4–6 (Paul's training)**. Paul becomes Jude's pupil **in Book Two** (not before). Structure: by custom six spells and three cantrips are "owed" an initiate; Jude gives two cantrips (mending, light) and five spells (sleep, protection, detect magic, identify, shield), withholding the sixth; recorded on parchment; a caster can be "tapped out"; shield is the reaction spell. Jude is an **evoker**; Paul reads as an **illusionist in the making** ("being unseen"). **Ch. 5** = *The First Cantrip* (mending learned; light not yet); **Ch. 6** frames the **wizard-assassin** hybrid. Render magic through effect, never exposed spell levels, slots, or bare game names.
- **`The_Sea_Principalities.md`** — load-bearing for **Ch. 6 (*The Wager*, Paul's opening reflection on the Toli)**. The five principalities: **Toli, Fairwind, Flotsom, Jetsom, Monmurg**; Toli set apart by old Suel blood, ruled by Prince Sacnon, still an equal of the five. Two non-voting powers: the **Plar of Hokar**, i.e. the **Duke of Berghof** (supplied the yellow powder), and the **Plar of Westkeep** (the northern reach along the **Hool** River). The **Amedio** jungle lies to the southeast (source of the Toli slave trade — spelling *Amedio*, not "Mediko").
- **`Helm_Island.md`** — background: the sahaugin are the **ancient enemy**; the hobgoblins are **Toli mercenaries from the mountains behind the Duchy of Berghof** (not a hobgoblin kingdom). The warding is the powder, not an enchantment.
- **`The_Homunculi.md`** — Paul's self-built homunculus (heartstone/mindstone, *lapis philosophorum*, mending-repair) is a marker of his raw talent referenced as his training begins.

## Politics & institutions carried in

- **Gradsul** (Duke of, Keoland) runs the **blockade** and is the Hold's oldest enemy; **Berghof** supplied the powder and the hobgoblin mercenaries — keep the *maker vs. blockader* distinction. **Zafar Azane**, **Lady Jamis's brother**, runs Gradsul's **Office of Forbidden Magic** (lithomancy / Soul Gem authority) — the shadow over Lord Jamis's household. The **Arcane Order** (Port Toli) guards Suel-era knowledge.

## Characters most in play

Jude, Paul, Jamis, Jeon, the alchemist **Albashon** (Ch. 4 briefly, Ch. 7), with Fairwind, Gouge, Folsom, Merrick, and Tyrus referenced. Check against `../../characters/`. Hold to: **Jeon** (never "Geon"); narration uses real names; **Gouge** is Jamis's knife-man, referenced by Jude in Ch. 5 as the road Paul must not walk.

## Open threads Book Two inherits (do not silently close)

- **Fairwind is not confirmed dead** — his body never washed ashore; (Jamis's earlier on-page refusal to have him assassinated was cut in the Ch. 1 recast; it survives only in the archived old Ch. 3.) He knows Jude for one of Jamis's agents.
- **The Sea Ghost** (Cain Toli's ship) is still at large; the **Redshore smuggling run** Owen is recruiting for (Gouge, Merrick, Tyrus's boat) is a live job.
- **The manner of combining** the powder — the central mystery — ties **Grayson's formulae**, the alchemist's notes, **Zafar Azane**, and Berghof's territories together (Ch. 7 opens this).
- **The black iron mask** (Identify thread) and the **Toli artifact / turtle dragons** remain unexplained.
