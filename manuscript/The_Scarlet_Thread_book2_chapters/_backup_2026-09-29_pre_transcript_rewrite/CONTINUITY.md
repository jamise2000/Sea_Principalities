# Book Two — Continuity Dependencies

A reference index, not an in-world artifact. Book Two shares its world, characters, and canon with Book One; the authoritative ledgers still live at the project root (`../../worldbuilding/`, `../../characters/`) and govern both books. This file only points to the specific canon Book Two leans on, so a continuity pass on these eight chapters knows what to check without re-reading all of Book One. When an edit here changes an established fact, update the root ledger in the same pass — this file is an index, not a second source of truth.

- **Transcripts:** Book Two is drawn from the eleven sessions listed in `../../transcripts/book2/transcripts.lst` (the tail of `Wealsun/3rd/` — Paul briefs Jeon, Jude travels to Jamis's office, Jude's conversation with Jamis and its end — plus all of `Wealsun/4th/`). ## Worldbuilding this book depends on

- **`The_Anti-Sahaugin_Powder.md`** — load-bearing for **Ch. 8 (*Copper and Sulfur*)**. Hold to: the powder is copper and sulfur combined by a lost method; **far more dangerous to water-breathers than to air-breathers, but NOT harmless to either** — diluted in the sea it is safe to swim, but the **raw powder is deadly to inhale** (Paul's mother's warning, Book One Ch. 6, Ch. 31; Paul warned Tyrus, Book One Ch. 38). The alchemist's "no harm at all" is reframed as diluted-in-water safety, with **Paul supplying the inhalation caveat**. Figures: one barrel ≈ ten square miles for ~ten days; twenty-five barrels were on the Helm, the survivors rafted home with **four** (Ch. 2); the recovered sample is ~twenty years old. Not ordinarily explosive, but volatile under pressure or after aging in seawater.
- **`magic_system.md`** — load-bearing for **Ch. 4–6 (Paul's training)**. Paul becomes Jude's pupil **in Book Two** (not before). Structure: spells "owed" a new initiate (six, plus three cantrips for Paul), recorded on parchment, a caster can be "tapped out," Shield is the reaction spell. Jude is an **evoker** (fire bolt his signature); Paul reads as an **illusionist in the making** ("the arts of being unseen" — Light-as-blinding-trick, Invisibility, Sleep-over-killing). **Ch. 5** = *The First Cantrip*; **Ch. 6** frames the **wizard-assassin** hybrid archetype. Render magic through effect, never exposed spell levels, slots, or bare game names.
- **`The_Sea_Principalities.md`** — load-bearing for **Ch. 7 (*What Paul Knew of the Toli*)**. The five principalities: **Toli, Fairwind, Flotsom, Jetsom, Monmurg**; Toli set apart by old Suel blood, ruled by Prince Sacnon, still an equal of the five. Two non-voting powers: the **Duke of Berghof** (supplied the yellow powder) and **Westkeep** (the northern reach along the **Hool** River). The **Amedio** jungle lies to the southeast (source of the Toli slave trade — spelling *Amedio*, not "Mediko").
- **`Helm_Island.md`** — background: the sahaugin are the **ancient enemy**; the hobgoblins are **Toli mercenaries from the mountains behind the Duchy of Berghof** (not a hobgoblin kingdom). The warding is the powder, not an enchantment.
- **`The_Homunculi.md`** — Paul's self-built homunculus (heartstone/mindstone, *lapis philosophorum*, mending-repair) is a marker of his raw talent referenced as his training begins.

## Politics & institutions carried in

- **Gradsul** (Duke of, Keoland) runs the **blockade** and is the Hold's oldest enemy; **Berghof** supplied the powder and the hobgoblin mercenaries — keep the *maker vs. blockader* distinction. **Zafar Azane**, **Lady Jamis's brother**, runs Gradsul's **Office of Forbidden Magic** (lithomancy / Soul Gem authority) — the shadow over Lord Jamis's household. The **Arcane Order** (Port Toli) guards Suel-era knowledge.

## Characters most in play

Jude, Paul, Jamis, Jeon, Owen Black, the alchemist (Ch. 8), with Fairwind and Gouge referenced. Check against `../../characters/`. Hold to: **Jeon** (never "Geon"); narration uses real names; **Gouge** is Jamis's knife-man, referenced by Jude in Ch. 5 as the road Paul must not walk.

## Open threads Book Two inherits (do not silently close)

- **Fairwind is not confirmed dead** — his body never washed ashore; Jamis keeps him **off the kill-on-sight list** (Ch. 3), believing he may yet turn. He knows Jude for one of Jamis's agents.
- **The Sea Ghost** (Cain Toli's ship) is still at large; the **Redshore smuggling run** Owen is recruiting for (Gouge, Merrick, Tyrus's boat) is a live job.
- **The manner of combining** the powder — the central mystery — ties **Grayson's formulae**, the alchemist's notes, **Zafar Azane**, and Berghof's territories together (Ch. 8 opens this).
- **The black iron mask** (Identify thread) and the **Toli artifact / turtle dragons** remain unexplained.
