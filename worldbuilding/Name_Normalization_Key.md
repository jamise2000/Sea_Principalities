# Name Normalization Key

The canonical-spelling authority for *Journey of the Stone*, and a decoder for how the raw session transcripts (audio diarization) mangle names and words. Use it when expanding transcripts to prose and to keep the manuscript, worldbuilding, and character files consistent. The verbatim transcript bodies are left as the raw record (per CLAUDE.md), except the two documented name edits noted at the foot of this file; this key is the authority for what the canonical form *is*. Compiled from a review of the working-list transcripts (Book Two, 3rd–6th Wealsun) and author rulings.

## The Toli distinction (do not conflate)

Three separate things share the word "Toli"; keep them distinct, and when connecting them, spell it out ("Cain Toli, of the Toli family of Port Toli"):

1. **Cain Toli** — an **individual**: the antagonist who wields the Orb of the Dragon Turtle. He answers to Sacnon Toli.
2. **the Toli** — a **family/house** of pure-Suel descent originating from Port Toli. Its head is **Prince Sacnon Toli**. Cain Toli is of this family.
3. **Port Toli** — a **city** on Jerlea Bay, the Toli seat.

**Sacnon Toli** is the **Prince of Port Toli** and **Cain Toli's leader** — the power Cain acts under. (Not to be confused with **Secundforth**, Viscount of Burle in mainland Salinmoor, a separate house; the transcripts' "Sconforth"/"Sikondforth" = Sacnon.)

## Houses and blood — the Azani thread (foreshadowing, held back in-world)

- **Azani** is the Suel family name of **Lady Jamis** and her brother **Zafar Azane** ("Azane" is the common rendering of the same house).
- **Paul** is (through his probable father Grayson Jamis, son of Lady Jamis) of Azani blood — **but he does not know it.** When the alchemist **Albashon** assumes Paul is kin to Zafar Azane and Lady Jamis and asks whether he uses "the name **Azani**," that is **foreshadowing**, not a fact Paul holds. Keep Paul's lineage a thing he guards or has not yet learned.

## The anti-sahaugin powder — origin (settled)

- The powder is **copper sulfate** — but it is **never named so** in dialogue; the alchemist **Albashon** calls it by the old Latin **vitriolum** (blue vitriol), or "copper and sulfur, combined in a way few know." When he mentions "sulfur and iron" he means **iron sulfate** (green vitriol), a different and more human-toxic salt. It was **invented and mass-produced by the Duke of Berghof**; **some mistakenly assume the Fieraxians made it** (the transcripts' "Phyraxian" = "Fieraxian"). Supply was **cut off ~40 years ago** (not common knowledge); about **25 barrels** remain to the Prince of Monmurg (**21 on the island** + **4 rafted home**). (Full ledger: `worldbuilding/The_Anti-Sahaugin_Powder.md`.)

## Edited transcript bodies (documented exceptions to "leave as raw record")

Only two verbatim transcript bodies have been edited, both to disambiguate a name; all other bodies remain the raw record. Both are logged in `transcripts/manuscript_divergences.md`.

**1. `Owen_shows_his_face_6th_Wealsun`** — the transcript where **Torus (NPC) is introduced** while also referencing **Tyrus (PC)** (the homophone trap):

- **Torus (NPC)** — the man in the cabin (was "Taurus"/"Tyrius"/"this guy Tyrus"): lines 41, 54, 75, 77, 88, 101.
- **Tyrus (PC)** — kept as Tyrus: the new boat Owen acquired for him (17, 18, 21), the party waiting for him (28), Folsom's "Tyrus is a good friend of mine" (110), and Owen's line 106.

**2. `Jude_in_Alchemy_shop_4th_Wealsun`** — the alchemist's name normalized from **Varen/Viren → Albashon** (lines 1 and 32). Line 32 stays a garbled sentence in the raw record; only the name token was changed.

The cast files and the `.lst` indexes already use canonical spellings.
